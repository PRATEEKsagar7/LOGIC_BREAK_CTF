THE UNKNOWN PERSON

Before the modern web became part of everyday life, sharing information between
different computer systems was difficult.

While working at a European research organization, one computer scientist proposed
a system that could connect information through documents and links.

His idea introduced a way to use hypertext together with addresses and communication
rules so that information could be accessed across different computers.

The first website and web server were created as part of this work.

What started as a solution for researchers eventually became the foundation of the
World Wide Web used around the world today.

The person behind this work became one of the most important figures in the history
of the web.
