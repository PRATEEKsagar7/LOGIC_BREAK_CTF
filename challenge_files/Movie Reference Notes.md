## Movie Reference Notes

This story follows a small team hired to enter a highly controlled virtual environment and influence the decisions of a powerful businessman.

Instead of fighting enemies directly, the team uses carefully designed artificial worlds where each level exists inside another. Time behaves differently in each layer, making every mistake more dangerous.

The main character is an experienced specialist who is struggling with memories of his past. During the mission, those memories begin interfering with the artificial world and putting the entire team at risk.

The team must complete the mission before the deeper layers become unstable and they lose their way back to reality.

The story focuses on dreams, memory, perception, time, and the difficulty of knowing whether what you are experiencing is real.
